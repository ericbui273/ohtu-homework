# Daily scrum reflection

I had the daily scrums simulation with the OhtuBot (which worked perfectly in English btw). All of its responses, including some comfort words and an offer to help to solve the blocker together, are very supportive and show the spirit of shared responsibility. I think it is exactly how a daily scrum should be like.

In a daily scrum, everyone should feel comfortable enough to give actual update about their progress, especially if there are blockers. If there are problem that is bothering the member, they should leave the daily scrum feeling better and feeling that their problem gets easier when they share it with the team.
